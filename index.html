🍉Forgotten Fridge

Track your food. Reduce waste. Never forget what's in your fridge again.

About

Forgotten Fridge is an Android application that helps you keep track of what's in your kitchen even when you're not.

⬇ Download APK

Features

  • Keep an inventory of everything in your kitchen!
  • Track purchase and expiration dates to reduce waste
  • Create reminders so you don't forget about expiring food!
  • Searchable inventory!
  • Generate printable inventory reports
  • Share reports with friends, family or other applications

Installation

  1. Download the APK using the button above.
  2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device.
  3. Click to allow installation from unknown sources, if required.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. Launch the Forgotten Fridge application to begin tracking your food.
Note: Because this application is distributed outside of the Google Play Store, Android may display a security warning before installation. This is normal for manually installed applications.
